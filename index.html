About the Game

Boo Sort: Color Bottles Puzzle is a fun, addictive, and relaxing liquid sorting game designed to challenge your brain and help you unwind. Join Boo, the cute bunny companion, on an epic color-matching adventure!

Sort vibrant colored liquids into matching glass tubes, solve clever logic puzzles, and enjoy thousands of satisfying levels at your own pace without pressure.
